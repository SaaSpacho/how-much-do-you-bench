# Methodology

## Sample

Responses collected between 4 and 21 February 2025 from data and analytics
leaders. Respondents were recruited through four B2B panel providers,
identified in the export by `PID`.

| `PID` | Provider | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary panel | The bulk of the sample |
| 2 | Secondary panel | Used to fill the large-enterprise quota |
| 3 | Secondary panel | Used to fill the large-enterprise quota |
| 4 | Pilot | See below |

**Panel 4 is the soft launch.** It ran a week before fieldwork opened, against
an earlier version of the screener, so its respondents are not comparable to
the rest. It stayed in the export because the raw file is kept as delivered.

Published figures are computed on panels 1, 2 and 3. Every number the research
team has put in a deck excludes panel 4, and a figure that includes it will not
reconcile with anything previously reported.

## Screener

Respondents qualified on role and on organization revenue. Those who failed
were terminated during the screener and are not in this export at all -- the
file contains completes only.
