# Weighting

## The scheme

Rim weighting on industry and revenue band, targets taken from the Eurostat
structural business statistics for the countries in scope, capped at 4.

## Where it applies

It was built for the modelled market-sizing work in section 6 of the report,
and it is applied there and nowhere else.

**Counts of respondents are unweighted.** Every figure that is "how many
organizations said X" is a straight count of respondents, and applying the
weights to one would produce a number that is not an integer and does not
reconcile with the base. The weights are not in this export for that reason.
