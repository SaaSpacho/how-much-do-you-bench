# Survey documentation

Everything the research team keeps about the 2025 tooling survey. Pages are
written when someone asks and are not always tidied afterwards.

* `methodology.md` -- how the sample was drawn and who is in it
* `data_quality.md` -- what we throw away and why
* `codebook.md` -- question codes and how answers are stored
* `weighting.md` -- the weighting scheme, and where it does and does not apply
